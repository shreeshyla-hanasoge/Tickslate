Tickslate 0.1.0 design sheet

Every new surface in 0.1.0, drawn on the real grid before anyone builds it: the generic card, metric and chart, the loading, empty, error and stale states, and the editing controls. Each board appears at desktop width and at 390px, in the theme the switch above sets. Implementing stories build to these frames.

Colour, type

Card

<ts-card>SWideTallMLBannerXL
  • ChromeThe shared tile: --ts-surface, a 1px --ts-line border, --ts-radius and --ts-pad. The header holds the live dot, the label, the meta (‘synced 14:05’) and then the actions slot at the far end. On a tile narrower than 200px the meta shortens to ‘14:05’ or ‘Stale’ so the label keeps its room.
  • Slotsvalue renders like .big and shrinks with the tile. footer is one line of 11px muted text at the bottom. The body takes the rest (flex: 1; min-height: 0), so a slotted chart at height: 100% fills it at every size.
  • ScrollableOnly the body scrolls, and its last 28px fade out so a cut-off row reads as more to come. Recent signups (Tall) shows it. The header, value and footer stay put.
  • On phonesS and Tall stay half width; Wide, M and Banner fill the width. L and XL take the M footprint. The card has no layouts of its own per size, so its slotted content reflows into the box it gets.

Metric

<ts-metric>SWideM
  • SizesS: label, headline and the change on the bottom line. Wide: the same on the left, a sparkline on the right. M: the change beside the headline, a sparkline with its first and last dates, a target bar and a caption.
  • The change▲ or ▼ (hidden from screen readers), then the signed change with + or the true minus sign. Wide and M show the amount and the percentage; S shows the percentage. The compare label follows in muted text.
  • Colour follows goodWhenSignups rises with goodWhen ‘up’, so it is profit-coloured; Revenue falls, so it is loss-coloured. p95 latency falls with ‘down’, so the fall is profit-coloured, while Open tickets rises with ‘down’ and is loss-coloured. Active seats uses ‘neither’ and stays muted.
  • Sparkline and targetThe sparkline takes the change’s colour, fills its area at --ts-fill and marks the last point. The target bar is --ts-accent on a --ts-surface-2 track, 6px tall, with its share of the target above it.

Chart

<ts-chart>WideMLXL
  • SizesWide names the first series in the header and has no legend. M adds a legend row under the header. L and XL move the legend to a rail on the right, with each series’ last value.
  • SeriesUp to four, in --ts-series-1 to --ts-series-4. Lines are 2px; areas fill to zero at --ts-fill. Every series ends in a dot. A legend swatch is a bar for a line and a filled square for an area, so the kind never relies on colour alone.
  • Axesx labels at the start, middle and end. From M up, dashed --ts-line gridlines carry compact labels on the right. The zero line is dashed --ts-axis.
  • On this boardWide: one area (Daily signups) and one line (p95 latency, live). M: an area with a line, and four lines. L: an area with a line. XL: four series, one area and three lines, with range buttons. Two metric tiles fill the column beside L.

Data states

card · metric · chart · Today’s P&L
  • LoadingThe real label stays. --ts-surface-2 blocks follow each size’s layout, so content lands without a shift. A shimmer crosses them every 1.6 s and stops under reduced motion. The tile carries aria-busy.
  • EmptyThe widget’s emptyText, centred and muted: ‘No trades today’, ‘No data yet’, or ‘No data’ by default. A slot="empty" child replaces it and stays clickable.
  • ErrorA --ts-warn glyph and the message, centred in ink. The host’s error text when it set one, else ‘Couldn’t display this data’. Retry appears only when actions include ‘retry’.
  • StaleThe content stays. The dot turns --ts-warn and stops pulsing, and the meta reads ‘Stale · 3 min’ in --ts-warn. In every state the tile keeps its size.

Loading, every size

Metric, Today’s P&L, chart and card at each size they support. The card keeps its header and actions; only its body becomes a skeleton.

Empty, default text

Today’s P&L (M), chart (Wide), metric (S) and card (S): the same four tiles in every board below, so each state can be compared with the others.

Empty, slotted call to action

Host content in slot="empty". Its look is the host’s choice; these use .btn with --ts-accent text.

Error

Today’s P&L and the card show the host’s error text (‘Feed disconnected’); the chart and metric show the default for a view model that threw.

Error with Retry

The same tiles with actions including ‘retry’. Retry fires ts-action with action ‘retry’; the tile stays in the error view until data arrives.

Stale

Live data that hasn’t changed for 3 minutes (stale-after defaults to 2 minutes). The region’s name gains ‘stale’.

Editing the grid

<ts-grid> · built-in controls
  • Edit pillFloats at the bottom of the grid while editing: --ts-surface, a --ts-line border and --ts-shadow-lift. Done is the --ts-accent button. Add back (n) appears while tiles are hidden and opens its list above the pill. Every target is 44px.
  • Undo toastAn --ts-ink ground with --ts-surface text, so it reads as a message rather than a control. It sits 10px above the pill, shows for 6 s, pauses on hover or focus, and appears without sliding under reduced motion.
  • Edit layoutThe grid’s first Tab stop. Hidden until focused, then shown over the top-left corner of the grid with a 2px --ts-focus ring outside a --ts-bg ring. The board doesn’t move.
  • Empty board and pickerA profile with no tiles keeps a one-row board with a dashed outline, the message and Add back. The picker (P1) is a bottom sheet below 700px and a centred dialog above, with removed tiles listed first.

Editing, nothing hidden

Remove badge top left and resize handle bottom right, as the grid draws them today. Tiles rock gently while editing; the frames hold still. The pill holds Done alone.

Just removed a tile

Revenue was removed: its gap has closed, the toast offers Undo, and the pill gains Add back (1). On the phone the grid is taller than the screen, so the pill and toast float over the tiles.

Add back, list open

Two tiles are hidden. The list names them by label, and the first has keyboard focus. Choosing one restores it and moves focus to the restored tile.

Edit layout button, focused

Keyboard focus has just reached the grid. Enter starts editing; when editing ends, focus comes back here.

Empty board

Every tile on this profile was removed. The board stays one row tall, and Add back opens the same list as the pill. A host can replace the panel with slot="empty".

Widget picker, P1 preview

Opened from the pill in a later release. Search has focus. Removed tiles come first as restores; each size chip adds the widget at that size.
