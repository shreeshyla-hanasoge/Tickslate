### Changed

- `linePaths` draws at most `maxPoints` points, `2 × width` by default, so a host that passes a long series now gets a line with fewer points above that size. Each run of readings keeps its lowest and highest, and the first, last, low and high are exact; the scales and the zero line still come from every point. Pass `maxPoints: Infinity` to draw them all.
- Widgets that are `display: none`, or more than one screen from the viewport, skip their later renders and draw once, with the latest data, when they can be seen again. A tile always draws its first content, wherever it is and whenever its data arrives, so a far-off tile never shows a loading skeleton for data it has; only the renders after that wait, and a tile far off-screen can show its last content, not the newest, until it is seen. A printout draws every tile first, before the browser takes its snapshot. The margin of one screen is measured from the viewport: inside a scrolled panel (`overflow: auto`) a tile draws when it first reaches the panel's edge, a frame before it is seen, not a screen ahead; a host with `display: contents` gets no gating.
- `maxPoints` of `null` means the default, and a value below 4 draws 4 points (the first, the last, a low and a high) with one development warning; pass `Infinity` to draw every point.
- Every widget timer of a whole number of seconds (the session clock's second, the 15 s freshness refresh, the calendar's minute) now runs on one shared timer aligned to the second, which sleeps while the page is hidden.

### Internal

- RFC 0003 §23 deviations and additions: `linePaths` takes `maxPoints` as §3.1 lists; the widget base also time-slices a burst of renders (past 12 ms in one task the rest wait for the next, except while printing), so thirty tiles fed in one tick no longer make one long task. Periods that are not a whole number of seconds keep their own `setInterval`.
- The shared ticker (`src/elements/ticker.js`) counts beats per subscriber, waits for the next second boundary before it starts, and stops its timer and its listener with the last subscriber.
- The session clock asks the calendar once per open or close, per candle and per day instead of every second.
- `dropIndex` lays the other tiles out once and tries the held tile at each slot (incremental placement, `createPlacer` in `src/core/place.js`), remembers the slots for the same tiles, and `<ts-grid>` reuses its last answer while the pointer stays in the same cell of the board (the pointer's cell is floored to the cell it is in before the slot is chosen, as before). About 0.1 ms at 100 tiles.
- `<ts-grid>` mounts without `ResizeObserver` (it listens for the window's `resize` instead) and without `MutationObserver`; a widget needs no `IntersectionObserver`.
- Today's P&L skips sorting readings that already arrive in time order.
- `test/browser/fixtures/widgets.html` has no `IntersectionObserver`, so the specs that read every tile of its long page keep seeing drawn tiles (the gating itself runs on every engine in `perf.spec.mjs`, on a page that keeps the observer); `freshness.spec.mjs` now expects the one shared 1 s interval.
- Tests: `test/ticker.test.mjs`, the downsampling and 500,000-reading cases in `test/chart.test.mjs`, the dropIndex benchmark and observer guards in `test/layout.test.mjs`, and `test/browser/perf.spec.mjs` (30 tiles, all in the render zone, at 4x CPU throttling on Chromium; on every engine: 200 mount/unmount cycles, hidden and distant widgets, a distant tile fed after the observer spoke, a printout of 30 waiting tiles, `display: contents`, and the drag memo). `TICKSLATE_PERF_SECONDS=60` runs the full 60 s window.
