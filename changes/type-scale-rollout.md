### Changed

- Every built-in widget and control now sizes its text from the rem type tokens (`--ts-text-*`, `--ts-label-size`) instead of fixed px, so a reader's larger default text size reaches all of them, as it already reached the shared tile text, `<ts-today-pnl>` and `<ts-equity-curve>`. At a 16px root and the default tokens nothing changes size, and a `html { font-size: 62.5% }` page is unchanged, because each token has a px floor. The one exception is the rebalance glyph of `<ts-capital-funding>`, which was 15px and is now 16px (the token it snaps to).
- At compact density, or at a larger root font size, a tile that cannot fit the row under its headline now hides it instead of cutting the headline: the change row of `<ts-metric>`, the from and to row of `<ts-session-clock>` `S`, the line under the stat of `<ts-edge-stats>` `S`, the status line of `<ts-capital-funding>` `S`, the text under the bar of `<ts-progress>`, and the Best and Worst stats of `<ts-pnl-calendar>` `Banner`. Their words stay in the tile's accessible name. A default dashboard hides nothing.
- Under a reader's text spacing, the next close of the `<ts-session-clock>` `Banner` header gives way with an ellipsis before the clock does, the times beside its lanes take the room they need instead of spilling past the tile edge, and the words under the bar of `<ts-progress>` `S` are no longer squeezed by their own box.

### Internal

- Every px font size in 14 element files (`card`, `metric`, `series-chart`, `table`, `watchlist`, `open-positions`, `pnl-calendar`, `edge-stats`, `capital-funding`, `progress`, `picker`, `grid-controls`, and two the RFC's list of twelve left out: `layout-list` and the scrub read-out in `scrub.js`) became `var(--ts-text-*, max(<px>, <rem>))`; the figures capped by the tile's width keep their shape with the token as the cap, and a label's `10px` fallback in `table.js` and `picker.js` is the token's own value. `session-clock.js` changed only for its `sec` row and the two layout rules above, to keep the merge with `market-presets-and-breaks` small.
- `test/density.test.mjs` scans every file in `src/elements` and every preset, not only the converted ones: no `font-size` or `font` in px, no `10px` label fallback, every fallback equal to its token, every cqi-capped figure capped by a token, and an inventory of the tokens each file reads. It also checks that each widget marks the row under its headline `sec`. The files left for this story are no longer asserted to be untouched.
- `test/browser/density.spec.mjs` has no list of known cut tiles: it fails if any gallery tile is cut at compact density, with the text-spacing overrides, or both, and runs the same check on every tile of the fixture page (all the built-in widgets, light and dark, desktop and phone widths). The check reads a shortened text as ending at the box that carries its ellipsis, and lets the line under a stat (`.sub`) be shortened, as `<ts-edge-stats>` builds it to be. `test/capital-funding.test.mjs` expects the `sec` class the status line now carries.
- Deviation from RFC 0004 §2.3: `layout-list.js` and `scrub.js` are converted too, because the widened scan covers every file in `src/elements`.
- Deviation: a row marked `sec` that its widget gives its own `display` (`.delta` in `metric.js`) restates the snug tier in the widget's styles, because a widget's rules come after the shared ones and win at equal specificity.
