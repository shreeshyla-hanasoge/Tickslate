### Changed

- Every built-in widget and control now sizes its text from the rem type tokens (`--ts-text-*`, `--ts-label-size`) instead of fixed px, so a reader's larger default text size reaches all of them, as it already reached the shared tile text, `<ts-today-pnl>` and `<ts-equity-curve>`. At a 16px root and the default tokens nothing changes size, and a `html { font-size: 62.5% }` page is unchanged, because each token has a px floor. The one exception is the rebalance glyph of `<ts-capital-funding>`, which was 15px and is now 16px (the token it snaps to).
- At compact density, or at a larger root font size, a tile that cannot fit the row under its headline now hides it instead of cutting the headline: the change row of `<ts-metric>`, the from and to row of `<ts-session-clock>` `S`, the line under the stat of `<ts-edge-stats>` `S`, the status line of `<ts-capital-funding>` `S`, the trade count and scope under the four stats of `<ts-edge-stats>` `Wide`, the text under the bar of `<ts-progress>`, and the Best and Worst stats of `<ts-pnl-calendar>` `Banner`. Their words stay in the tile's accessible name. A default dashboard hides nothing.
- A `<ts-pnl-calendar>` `Banner` narrower than 700px on a 150px row (a phone's, or a tablet's two columns) also drops the Best and Worst stats and the weekday names, because with them the year was squeezed to a few pixels and its labels were cut. This reaches a comfortable phone `Banner` too, which drew the same squeezed year.
- Under a reader's text spacing, the next close of the `<ts-session-clock>` `Banner` header gives way with an ellipsis before the clock does, the times beside its lanes take the room they need instead of spilling past the tile edge, and the words under the bar of `<ts-progress>` `S` are no longer squeezed by their own box.

### Internal

- Every px font size in 14 element files (`card`, `metric`, `series-chart`, `table`, `watchlist`, `open-positions`, `pnl-calendar`, `edge-stats`, `capital-funding`, `progress`, `picker`, `grid-controls`, and two the RFC's list of twelve left out: `layout-list` and the scrub read-out in `scrub.js`) became `var(--ts-text-*, max(<px>, <rem>))`; the figures capped by the tile's width keep their shape with the token as the cap, and a label's `10px` fallback in `table.js` and `picker.js` is the token's own value. `session-clock.js` changed only for its `sec` row and the two layout rules above, to keep the merge with `market-presets-and-breaks` small.
- `test/density.test.mjs` scans every file in `src/elements` and every preset, not only the converted ones: no `font-size` or `font` in px, no `10px` label fallback, every fallback equal to its token, every cqi-capped figure capped by a token, and an inventory of the tokens each file reads. It also checks that each widget marks the row under its headline `sec`. The files left for this story are no longer asserted to be untouched.
- `test/density.test.mjs` finds a px size written any way: in a stylesheet, an inline `style="…"` or `style='…'`, a `cssText` string, a `fontSize` property, an SVG `font-size` attribute with no unit, a `pt` size, any capitalisation, and a `var()` whose fallback is not a type token. Each form is planted in a scratch copy of an element file and must fail the scan, and the forms the files may use must pass.
- `test/browser/density.spec.mjs` inspects every text node in every shadow root, not only the headline, label and figure (a note, a footer, a month label or the words under a bar included), and runs the fixture page at compact density alone as well as with the text-spacing overrides, light and dark, desktop and phone widths. A real `<ts-grid>` at 700px and 800px (two and three columns, 150px rows) runs the same check. It reads a shortened text as ending at the box that carries its ellipsis, and lets a label, a name, a note and the line under a stat (`.sub`) be shortened. `test/capital-funding.test.mjs` expects the `sec` class the status line now carries. The fixture page gives each figure a 170px floor, so a tile with no content of its own (`<ts-progress>` `S`) is drawn at a phone's width and not at 44px.
- Follow-up, layout work a tier cannot do (a tier hides a row at a content height, and a comfortable tile of the same height must keep it); each has a `test.fixme` in the spec that asserts the tile is clean: `<ts-progress>` `Wide` under the text-spacing overrides on a compact tile (the figure's 1.5 line height pushes the words under the bar out at 150px rows, and pushes the bar itself out of a 120px tile, which the text check does not see), the `S` figure of `<ts-today-pnl>` on a 249px tile under the overrides (it is sized at 15cqi for 304px), and the last month label of the `<ts-pnl-calendar>` `Banner` at 800px under the overrides.
- The Internal note of `changes/density-and-text-scaling.md` that the other widgets "still draw their small text in px" is false once this lands; drop it at release prep. `docs/theming.md` already says so.
- Deviation from RFC 0004 §2.3: `layout-list.js` and `scrub.js` are converted too, because the widened scan covers every file in `src/elements`.
- Deviation: a row marked `sec` that its widget gives its own `display` (`.delta` in `metric.js`) restates the snug tier in the widget's styles, because a widget's rules come after the shared ones and win at equal specificity.
